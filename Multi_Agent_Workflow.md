# Multi-Agent Research Workflow

## Workflow
Research Objective -> Coordinator -> Multiple Agents -> Results -> Consensus

## Expanded Agents
- DiscoveryAgent: Analyzes DiscoveryReport, creates ResearchTask
- PatternAgent: Extracts patterns from experiments and results
- StrategyArchitectAgent: Designs strategy templates
- ExperimentManagerAgent: Plans experiment execution
- BenchmarkAgent: Evaluates strategy performance
- ResearchHistorianAgent: Tracks research history

## Collaboration
- ResearchTeamCoordinator: assign tasks, collect results, resolve conflicts, merge conclusions
- ResearchDebateSystem: arguments, counter-arguments, voting, final decision

## Decision Flow
Goal -> Director -> Form Team -> Delegate -> Debate -> Vote -> Decision -> Knowledge Update
