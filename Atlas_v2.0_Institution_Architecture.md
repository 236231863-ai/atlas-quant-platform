# Atlas Quant Platform v2.0 - Artificial Research Institution

## Overview
The final evolution: an AI autonomous research organization with governance, departments, scientist careers, publications, peer review, and asset management.

## Architecture
```
Artificial Research Institution
  Governance -> Rules + Compliance
  Departments -> Probability/Optimization/Strategy/Risk/ML/Discovery
  Career -> Intern -> Research -> Senior -> Principal -> Director
  Publication -> Papers + Reports + Articles
  Review Committee -> 5 specialized reviewers
  Asset Management -> Strategies/Models/Datasets/Knowledge
  Institution Director -> Central coordination
  Dashboard -> Institution data
